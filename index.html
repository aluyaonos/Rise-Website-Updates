We bring global wealth opportunities closer to you

Built to make global wealth more accessible

Our mission at Rise is to connect our users to the best wealth creating opportunities in the world. Our goal is to help them create wealth and achieve their financial goals.

The idea for Rise sparked in 2014, when we saw how inflation, currency shocks and devaluation made it harder for people in emerging markets to grow their wealth and reach their financial goals. We built Rise to connect users to dollar-denominated investments in developed markets, making stronger wealth-building opportunities more accessible while helping people make more informed financial decisions.
