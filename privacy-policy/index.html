At Rise, we believe managing your money should come with peace of mind. That includes knowing how your personal information is collected, used, stored, and protected whenever you interact with our products and services.

Your privacy matters to us, and we're committed to being transparent about how we handle the information you share. This Privacy Policy explains the types of information we may collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

By using our website, mobile application, or other services, you acknowledge that your personal information may be processed as described in this Privacy Policy, where permitted by applicable law.

About This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Rise may collect, use, disclose, and protect personal information when you visit our website, create an account, explore investment opportunities, or use our services.

It applies to information collected through our digital platforms, customer support channels, marketing communications, and other interactions with Rise.

Some services may have additional privacy notices or requirements depending on the products you use, your location, and applicable financial regulations.

We encourage you to read this policy carefully so you understand how your information is handled.

Who This Policy Applies To

This policy applies to individuals who:

  • Visit or interact with the Rise website.
  • Register for or maintain a Rise account.
  • Explore, create, or manage investment plans.
  • Contact our customer support team.
  • Subscribe to our emails or other communications.
  • Participate in surveys, promotional activities, or research.

Different privacy requirements may apply depending on your country of residence and the services available to you.

Information We Collect

To provide financial services and maintain a secure platform, we may collect different types of personal information.

The information collected will generally depend on how you interact with Rise and which services you choose to use.

Personal Information You Provide

When you create an account or use certain services, we may request information such as:

  • Full name and date of birth.
  • Email address and phone number.
  • Residential address and country of residence.
  • Government-issued identification information.
  • Employment details or financial information.
  • Bank account and payment information.
  • Investment preferences and financial goals.

We may also collect information you provide when communicating with our team, completing surveys, or updating your account details.

Financial and Investment Information

When you use our investment services, we may process information relating to your financial activity.

This may include your transaction history, investment contributions, withdrawals, account balances, selected investment plans, and other information required to operate your account.
